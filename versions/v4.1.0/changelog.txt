# Prominence II: Hasturian Era
**v4.1.0 - Simply Swords and Bows Update**

This is the awaited Simply Swords update! It fully reworks many Simply Swords and adds a couple of new ones. Your build may need to be adjusted as this update will most likely make every build guide obsolete. There is now a handful of Simply Bows, which have their own unique abilities, which all can be used with your Archery books!
