# Prominence II: Hasturian Era
**v4.0.3 - Various fixes once again and Wizard rework**

This update removes Frostiful again and makes Wizard Progression more linear. Open Parties and Claims now also has a UI to make allying and making parties easier.

## New Party UI!
- Press "P" to open the Opac UI and invite friends and allies there.

## Changes
- Added more Compatibility to Ashen with mods such as JustHammers and Better Nether Items
- Removed Frostiful, it was added due to a mistake, sorry
- All Spell school Robes can now be crafted out of the Wizard Robes, making progression more linear
- Wands can now be made with the Wizard Staff and continuously upgraded, so you can keep your Staff and make it into Ekavar
- Added the Ability to manage who can use Waystones in claims, now being able to set to noone, allies, everyone and Party
- Removed an obsolete armor from the combat and Gear Questline
- Made the Kill the Ender Dragon quest in the Main Questline also give the Advancement in case the player somehow didn't get it
- Belt now takes Lanterns again
- Parties quest in the Getting started Questline now Mentions being able to use P to open the Party interface
- Fixed Dark Armor still being shown as needed for the Warrior Tier 3 Questline
- Made the Simply Swords Questline Progress-able without having to smelt in at least one Simply Sword
- Various small tweaks in the Questbook not worth mentioning
  
## Mods
- Added Mods: Opac UI
- Removed Mods: Frostiful
- Updated Mods: S-Lib, Smart Tooltip Scroll, Custom Tooltip API, Alm's Recipe Additions
- Added Resourcepacks: Ashen JustHammers Support, Ashen BetterNether Items Support
