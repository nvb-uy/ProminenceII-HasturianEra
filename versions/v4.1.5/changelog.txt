# Prominence II: Hasturian Era
**v4.1.5 - Halloween Event & Removal of Deeper and Darker and Bumblezone**

This update unlocks the Halloween Questline that yields great rewards with a seasonal boss. This update also brings 19 new mods that have been in Prominent before, so not much of a change for the player. Tons of QoL and fixes. You can even now disable the fade in when switching dimension.
Vaaz got a thematic look, there is a new Seasonal Mythic boss and so much more mentioned down below, like a Wizard rebalancing!

This is it! Bumblezone and Deeper and Darker have been removed! 
You don't need to do anything before updating, if you have already left the Dimension and have moved your items. It is impossible to retrieve them once you update. If you are still in the dimension you will be set into the overworld. In the worst case you die in the overworld, with your grave being there too. MAKE A BACKUP BEFORE UPDATING REGARDLESS!! We cannot assist you once you have updated and lost your items, so a Backup is essential.

Your options will reset!

## Changes
- Changed Speed in Vaaz so now you can walk faster there
- Xaeros and Opac was updated! This should not cause any issues, but you may need to check your options again incase one of your teammates no longer has access to specific parts of your base now.
- Essential mod Analytics sharing is now disabled by default - you will get a pop up ingame asking you to agree or not, this is their decision
- Options screen should be limited to 21/9 ratio (for people with ultrawides, weirdos)
- Keepinventory now works in Assault Vaaz, so dying at the same time as the boss should no longer void your entire inventory
- There is now another level cap before the Dragon so you cannot overlevel

## Balancing
Healing was a bit too strong for all classes and made Classes who depended on it not shine as bright. Bursting vitality has always been a bit too strong since it's fix
- Lowered Battle Rouse from 3 to 2 as it largely made everyone a bit more Tanky that intended
- Lowered Charm of Bursting Vitality to 1

Mages have been lackluster in all aspects of the game. Hard to start with, not worth later on, compared to other classes. This is the first step towards fixing this. Wizards are some of the most played classes and deserve a lot more love.
