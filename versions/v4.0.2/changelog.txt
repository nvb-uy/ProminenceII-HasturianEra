# Prominence II: Hasturian Era
**v4.0.2 - Various fixes and brand new Supernova**

This update introduces various fixes, including fixes for mythic bosses scaling.

It also introduces **numerous more trades** for early to mid game, and includes a trade for the Unholy Hourglass.

All FTB Teams will be disbanded because of bugs caused by being on a team that break progression! Don't worry though, you won't lose any quest progress.
Please use open parties and claims.

**YOUR OPTIONS WILL BE RESET!**

## Artifacts
- Added brand new Supernova model

## Vaaz NPC Vendors
- Added molten remnants trades for S'kellak
- Fixed many trades missing objects

## Mythic Challenges
- Mythic 16+ can now be done without a team of 5 people, but it will scale the same way as a 5 player team.
- Fixed Arachne's health scaling being broken
- Nerfed gu'mas' health to be singificantly lower
- Slightly nerfed Ama'gul's hp to be lower
- Affix adds (cultists, aberrations, withers, chupacabras) now spawn with 25% of the health they used to
- Affix adds no longer stack infinitely
- Spiderlings, smaller golems and other spawns from bosses no longer stay after the challenge ends

## Bosses
- Ama'gul now sends a message when hitting a clone, making it more accessible to people with no audio

## Accessibility Features
- Added Arachnophobia mode for Arachne(turns into a robot)

## Other Changes
- Added more Compatibility to Ashen with mods such as Builders Wands, the Alloy Forge and Oxidized
- Fixed a Critical memory leak with Copycats
- Failing a Mythic Challenge now rerolls the boss on your returned hourglass, same as winning, and neither can land on the boss you just fought
- Tripping and falling inside a Mythic Challenge no longer kills you outright, it now counts as a challenge death
- The Questbook should overall be less prone to greyed out quests
- Fixed some compat with the drills from Modern Industrialization still giving disabled ores from other mods
- Titanium drillalso now gives uraninite
- Steel drill now gives silver ore 
- Added missing Tags to Garlic and Water artichoke from Enchanted:Witchcraft
- Added Tags to the Chili Pepper so it's compatible with the Chile Pepper
- Removed % Hp Scaling on the Darkin Scythe
- Lowered Invading Forlorn Spawn
- Removed Dragons Breath Trim due to bugs
- Fixed the Stabby Stab and Meteorite Compass quest in the Applied Energistics Questline
- Made a few more quests properly optional
- Fixed Arachne having twice the stats
- Fixed Pain Cycle 3 being obtainable from villagers but not being able to be applied
- Completely removed Spell infinity from trading and the enchanting level as it is no longer needed for casting spells
- Fixed some trades as they were too late or too early, such as a few Simply swords, Armor pieces
- Added Houglass Trade to Alm, so players can buy a new one to level
- Fixed some smaller Crashes and issues
