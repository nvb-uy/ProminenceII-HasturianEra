# Prominence II: Hasturian Era
**v4.1.3 - Aquamirae Update and fixes**

This update largely updates mods as it was a bit neglected. Aquamirae finally gets an update so you can experience it like you see in those Tiktok edits
REMINDER ONCE MORE!!
We have decided that we are removing both Bumblezone and Deeper and Darker! This removal will happen in about 2-3 Updates! More info on when exactly and what to do will follow the coming weeks.
This is part of our debloating of Prominence as both dimensions don't serve a direct purpose. Honey items will be added to Vaaz trades in the same update as these mods are being removed.
This means any block from both of these mods, including their Dimension will be gone, once the mod is removed. Sorry to all the Bee fans and the singular Deep Dark fan, if you still exist!

We are currently aware of an issue causing mob spawn to be weird and are working on a fix. There is a temp fix in the Discord if you want to try it. It will most likely be fixed by next update.

## Changes
- New cooler EMI showcase for the Spell binding Table
- Lowered Artifact Damage on Ghaj, to 2% on the haste focused node and 4% on the damage focused node
- Lowered Emberlash Stacks to 6
- Lowered Emberlash Spell scaling from 0.38 to 0.25
- Added a Mod so Drekora can have a unified Hub

## Additions
- New Ice Maze Dimension coming with the Aquamirae Update! Check out the Explorers Journal for more info on how to get there and how to get back out
- Massive Shipbreaker Event in the Ice maze!
- Added more Quest info to the dimension and such
- Mobs from the Ice maze take less damage from normal weapons
- Cornelia may be a small bit harder, since armor and damage is less effective
- To combat this, Depth’s Fury governs your damage to them, and Depth’s Resilience governs how hard they hit you and how much of your armor counts
- Entire Biome looks cooler!
  
## Fixes
- Remove the Bloat Armors from EMI that were added by Aquamirae
- Fixed the Battlestandards being un-nerfed once again
- Hopefully fixed a bug with Modrinth giving you the wrong FTB mods
- Fixed a ton of issues from other mods, the game might run better now for most players in general
